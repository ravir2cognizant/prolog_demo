## Deferred — Pending OQ-012

RC-010 (GL Import) received 3 CNCs at the T-GATE sign-off (A-02, A-05, A-08). Endpoint design cannot proceed until OQ-012 is resolved.

**OQ-012:** What is the GL Import mechanism on a journal entry?
Questions requiring answers:
- What file format(s) are supported for GL import? (CSV, Excel, XML, proprietary?)
- What is the scope of a single import? (One journal entry, multiple entries, a batch?)
- Is the import operation synchronous (immediate response with results) or asynchronous (job submitted, status polled)?
- Who triggers the import — the GL accountant from the journal entry form, or a separate batch process?
- Is "GL Import" on the journal entry header a trigger for import, or a reference/link to an already-imported batch?

## Placeholder Endpoint Summary

The following is a placeholder only. Design will be completed when OQ-012 is resolved.
